Privacy Policy & Local Data Commitment

Our Architecture: Local-First, Zero-Surveillance, Privacy by Design

At Sidestick Priority, we respect your privacy. SceneryX does not track, collect, monetize, or sell your personal data, system identifiers, or flight simulator habits.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

  • No Data Harvesting: SceneryX does not collect, scrape, store, or sell any personal data, email addresses, or system identifiers.
  • No Hardware Fingerprinting: No unique device IDs or Windows registry user profiles are transmitted to any central tracking servers.
  • No Behavioral Analytics: Zero telemetry trackers, zero tracking pixels, and zero third-party advertising SDKs are embedded in the application.

2. Local Data Storage Architecture

All configuration settings, catalog indexes, pricing overrides, and performance benchmarks used by SceneryX are generated locally and stored strictly on your computer's local drive:

%APPDATA%\SceneryX\settings.json UI preferences, camera speeds, startup region
%APPDATA%\SceneryX\installed_airports.json Local scenery index cache
%APPDATA%\SceneryX\ratings.json User star ratings (1 to 5 stars)
%APPDATA%\SceneryX\custom_prices.json Manual price overrides
%APPDATA%\SceneryX\library_snapshot.json Delta tracking hashes for fast startup

None of these files are ever synced to cloud storage, remote databases, or external analytics endpoints.

SimBrief (Navigraph): SceneryX queries SimBrief's public API strictly using your public Pilot ID or Username to download active Operational Flight Plan (OFP) routes. We never request, process, or store your account password.

Gumroad (License Activation): Software activation keys are validated directly with Gumroad over encrypted HTTPS (api.gumroad.com). SceneryX never collects, handles, or stores any credit cards, banking details, or billing addresses.

Store Lookups (Flightsim.to, Orbx, Contrail): Clicking store links opens search URLs directly inside your default web browser without passing telemetry.
