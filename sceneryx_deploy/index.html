QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed, technical answers regarding SceneryX performance, architecture, and simulator compatibility.

How does SceneryX unify MSFS settings for both 2D displays and VR?
MSFS natively combines desktop and VR rendering parameters into buried sub-menus, requiring pilots to manually edit the UserCfg.opt file or endure multiple simulator restarts. SceneryX introduces an independent dual-profile system. You can establish crisp 4K desktop parameters (DLSS Quality, ultra shadows, high TLOD) for standard monitor flying, while maintaining a dedicated OpenXR configuration (foveated rendering, custom per-eye render resolution, motion reprojection toggles) for your VR headset. SceneryX synchronizes these settings safely with zero risk of config corruption and without requiring repeated simulator reboots.
How does SceneryX manage MSFS 2024 streamed packages versus local sceneries?
Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 stores streamed assets in cloud-managed StreamedPackages containers labeled with cryptic cryptographic GUIDs. The native simulator Content Manager gives you no clear distinction between what is cached on disk and what streams over the wire. SceneryX de-anonymizes these packages by inspecting simulator manifest caches. It clearly displays whether an airport is hosted on your local NVMe drive (Community or Official) or streamed from the cloud. This gives you absolute control to prioritize high-fidelity local payware over low-resolution streamed photogrammetry and prevents duplicate scenery collisions.
How does the automatic GSX Pro profile matching work?
SceneryX includes a specialized heuristic parser for GSX Pro configuration files. When you drag and drop any .ini or .zip profile into SceneryX, the engine reads the airport ICAO, terminal gates, parking positions, and author signature. It automatically differentiates between default Asobo airports and third-party payware editions (e.g. Flightbeam, Pyreegue, or iniBuilds), moving the profile into the exact folder path at %APPDATA%\Virtuali\GSX\MSFS. Furthermore, SceneryX runs a health audit to identify and eliminate obsolete, duplicate, or corrupted GSX profile files.
Why doesn't SceneryX include flight planning or airport charts?
We believe in doing one thing with world-class excellence rather than building mediocre imitations of established platforms. Tools like SimBrief and Navigraph already provide industry-leading navdata, flight dispatching, and georeferenced airport charts. Instead of bloating our binary with redundant charting code, SceneryX connects directly to your existing SimBrief account via API. We import your active Operational Flight Plan (OFP) to build the scenery flight corridor and telemetry benchmarks, keeping SceneryX ultra-lightweight, lightning-fast, and focused on scenery management and real-time simulator performance.
Does SceneryX modify my simulator installation files permanently?
No, absolutely not. SceneryX is built from the ground up on a 100% non-destructive architecture. We never modify core simulator game files, alter official binaries, or rename folders on your storage drives. Scenery prioritization is managed via isolated session Content.xml manifests, while Dynamic Smart LOD scaling operates exclusively in runtime memory through official SimConnect APIs. Closing SceneryX or clicking the "Restore All" button instantly restores your simulator to its factory configuration in under one second.
Is SceneryX fully compatible with complex airliners (Fenix, PMDG, FBW)?
Yes, study-level airliners (such as the Fenix A320/A321, PMDG 737/777, FlyByWire A380X, iniBuilds A300/A350, and TFDi MD-11) are where SceneryX provides the most dramatic benefits. High-fidelity airliners place heavy demands on your CPU MainThread for complex avionics, custom fly-by-wire physics, and dual EFBs. By dynamically scaling back Terrain LOD on short final and parking unneeded sceneries via SimBrief isolation, SceneryX frees crucial CPU headroom right when you disconnect the autopilot for manual flare and touchdown.
