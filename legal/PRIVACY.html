Privacy Policy & Local Data Commitment

Author & Licensor: Sidestick Priority • Software: SceneryX

OUR CORE COMMITMENT: ABSOLUTE PRIVACY FOR FLIGHT SIMULATOR PILOTS.

SceneryX operates on a strict Local-First, Zero-Surveillance architecture. We do not track, collect, monetize, or sell your personal data or flight simulator habits.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

  • No Data Harvesting: SceneryX does not collect, scrape, store, or sell any personal data, email addresses, or system identifiers.
  • No Hardware Fingerprinting: No unique device IDs or Windows registry user profiles are transmitted to any central tracking servers.
  • No Behavioral Analytics: Zero telemetry trackers, zero tracking pixels, and zero third-party advertising SDKs are embedded in the application.

2. Local Data Storage Architecture

All configuration files, scanned sceneries, and ratings are stored exclusively on your local disk:

%APPDATA%\SceneryX\settings.json UI preferences, camera speeds, startup region
%APPDATA%\SceneryX\installed_airports.json Local scenery index cache
%APPDATA%\SceneryX\ratings.json User star ratings (1 to 5 stars)
%APPDATA%\SceneryX\custom_prices.json Manual price overrides
%APPDATA%\SceneryX\library_snapshot.json Delta tracking hashes for fast startup

None of these files are ever synced to external servers or cloud repositories.

3. Third-Party APIs & External Services

3.1. SimBrief (Navigraph): SceneryX connects to SimBrief's public API strictly using your public Pilot ID or Username to download active Operational Flight Plan (OFP) routes. We never request, process, or store your account password.

3.2. Gumroad (License Activation): When verifying software activation keys, SceneryX communicates directly with Gumroad over encrypted HTTPS (api.gumroad.com). SceneryX never collects, handles, or stores any credit cards, banking details, or billing addresses.

3.3. Store Lookups (Flightsim.to, Orbx, Contrail): Clicking store links opens search URLs directly inside your default web browser without passing telemetry.

4. User Rights & Complete Data Deletion

You maintain 100% ownership and control over your data. To permanently erase all SceneryX data, simply delete the local folder:

%APPDATA%\SceneryX\

Deleting this folder immediately and permanently purges all local settings, indexes, ratings, and snapshots from your computer.
