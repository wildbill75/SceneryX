SceneryX synchronizes these settings safely with zero risk of config corruption and without requiring repeated simulator reboots.
Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 stores streamed assets in cloud-managed StreamedPackages containers labeled with cryptic cryptographic GUIDs. The native simulator Content Manager gives you no clear distinction between what is cached on disk and what streams over the wire. SceneryX de-anonymizes these packages by inspecting simulator manifest caches. It clearly displays whether an airport is hosted on your local NVMe drive (Community or Official) or streamed from the cloud. This gives you absolute control to prioritize high-fidelity local payware over low-resolution streamed photogrammetry and prevents duplicate scenery collisions.
SceneryX includes a specialized heuristic parser for GSX Pro configuration files. Furthermore, SceneryX runs a health audit to identify and eliminate obsolete, duplicate, or corrupted GSX profile files.
