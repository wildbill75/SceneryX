# SceneryX — Next-Generation MSFS Scenery & Collection Manager (BETA)

> **The ultimate companion app for Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020 & 2024).**  
> Visualize your entire airport collection on an interactive global map, audit your payware investment, compare prices across 15+ official stores in real-time, and manage your Community and StreamedPackages libraries effortlessly.

---

### 🌟 Why SceneryX?
As flight simmers, our scenery libraries grow rapidly over time: hundreds of handcrafted airports scattered across Community folders, Official packages, and cloud-streamed assets. It becomes nearly impossible to remember what sceneries you own, which airports have active GSX Pro profiles, where you've flown, or how much you've invested in your virtual world.

**SceneryX was built from the ground up to solve this.** It is a standalone, lightweight, zero-bloat desktop tool that automatically catalogs your simulators, extracts runway & geographical data, links your GSX ground service profiles, and gives you a panoramic view of your global flight sim footprint.

---

### 🚀 Key Features

#### 🗺️ Interactive Global 3D/2D Airport Map
* **Global Visualization:** Powered by Leaflet & MarkerCluster for ultra-smooth rendering of thousands of airports worldwide.
* **Smart Cluster Breakdowns:** Dynamic grouping by continent, region, and airport density.
* **Cinematic Camera Controls:** Fluid pan & zoom transitions with customizable speeds (*Fast*, *Smooth*, *Cinematic*) and default zoom depths (*Wide*, *Regional*, *Runway*).
* **Configurable Startup Focus:** Set your preferred initial viewport (*World*, *Western Europe*, *North America*, *Asia*, *Oceania*, etc.).
* **Instant Hover Cards:** Hover over any airport marker to inspect ICAO code, city, country, status (Payware, Freeware, Asobo), and active GSX profile presence.

#### 🔍 Deep Dual Simulator Scanning (MSFS 2020 & MSFS 2024)
* **Full Multi-Path Support:** Automatically detects local `Community` folders, `Official` packages, and MSFS 2024 `StreamedPackages`.
* **Custom Folder Management:** Add multiple scenery drives or custom add-on directories; name them and toggle their scan status with a single switch.
* **Precise Metadata Engine:** Reads layout definitions, airport coordinates, runways, elevation, and package identity.
* **GSX Pro Profile Auto-Matching:** Automatically scans `%APPDATA%\Virtuali\GSX\MSFS` and marks airports that have a custom `.ini` ground handling profile installed.

#### 💰 Collection Investment & Financial Dashboard
* **Total Spent Counter:** Live summary banner tracking your cumulative investment across all payware sceneries.
* **Multi-Currency Converter:** Switch seamlessly between **USD ($)**, **EUR (€)**, **GBP (£)**, and **AUD (A$)**; all values across the entire application update reactively.
* **Custom Price Overrides:** Manually adjust the price paid for any airport (sale discounts, bundles, gifts) directly inside the airport details drawer.
* **Country Investment Drawer:** Drill down by country to view total airports owned, money spent, and a dedicated clickable airport roster with instant map focus.

#### 🛒 Live Payware Store Price Comparator
* **15+ Flight Sim Stores Checked in Real-Time:** Query live availability and pricing across *simMarket, Orbx Direct, Flightbeam Studios, iniBuilds Store, Aerosoft Shop, France VFR, FlyTampa, FSDreamTeam, Jetstream Designs, LatinVFR, Pyreegue Dev Co., NZA Simulations, Drzewiecki Design, Contrail, Flightsim.to*.
* **Cheapest First Sorting:** Matching stores are automatically sorted from lowest price to highest price converted into your chosen currency.
* **Official Developer Studio Recognition (`👑 [OFFICIAL DEV]`):** Official developer stores and creators are highlighted with distinct golden badges.
* **Anti-False-Positive Filter:** Automatically filters out vintage/retro packages (e.g. *1935 Geneva*), landmark packs (e.g. *Paris Landmarks*), liveries, textures, sound packs, and older simulator versions (*FSX*, *P3D*, *X-Plane*).
* **Multi-Tab Workflow:** The store modal stays open when clicking external links so you can cross-check deals without interruption.

#### 📤 Multi-Format Exports & Backups
* **CSV Spreadsheet:** Export your collection into an Excel-, Google Sheets-, Volanta-, and Elevatex-compatible CSV.
* **JSON Full Backup:** Export complete raw structured metadata for database backup or third-party tool automation.
* **Scope Selection:** Export your entire catalog or only the sceneries currently visible based on active map filters.

#### 🖥️ Native Ergonomics & Multi-Language
* **Automatic Screen Resolution Adaptation:** Scales window dimensions smoothly on 1080p, 1440p (2K), and 4K displays.
* **Pixel-Perfect Centering:** Symmetrical margins taking into account the Windows taskbar.
* **4 Full Languages:** English, Français, Deutsch, and Español.

### 📦 Installation & Quick Start

1. **Download** the `SceneryX_v1.0.1_BETA.zip` archive.
2. **Extract** the folder anywhere on your PC (e.g. `C:\Tools\SceneryX` or your desktop).
3. **Launch** `SceneryX.exe`.
4. On first launch, SceneryX will automatically detect your MSFS installation paths. Click **Scan** to catalog your airports in seconds!

> **Note:** SceneryX is a standalone portable application. It does **NOT** require any installer, registry keys, or administrative permissions to run.

### 🔒 Privacy & Safety Guarantee
* **100% Local Processing:** SceneryX runs completely on your computer. It does not collect, track, or transmit any personal data, simulator telemetry, credentials, or analytical usage data.
* **Safe & Non-Destructive:** SceneryX operates in read-only mode for cataloging. It never modifies your simulator core files without your explicit confirmation.
* **No Background Bloat:** Zero background services, zero auto-launch bloatware.
